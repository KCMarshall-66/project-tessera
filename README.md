# Project Tessera

Project Tessera is a design/UX exploration built around **Tessera**, a fictional AI-assisted cybersecurity SaaS company. It exists as a sandbox for practicing and showcasing privacy/vendor-risk product design work: personas, empathy maps, prototypes, a design system, brand identity, and a fictional company profile.

## Concept

Tessera is a cybersecurity SaaS platform that uses AI to help large enterprises and their privacy teams manage, assess, and monitor third-party vendor risk — continuously, rather than through point-in-time questionnaires. The primary user is **Dana Vasquez**, a Vendor Risk Manager, supported by two analysts on her team. The broader persona suite also covers other Privacy Management roles (e.g. DPO, Privacy Counsel, Data Governance Lead, etc.) who interact with vendor risk data across the org.

## Repo structure

- [`personas/`](personas) — Persona profiles for Dana Vasquez, her analysts, and the wider Privacy Management persona suite.
- [`empathy-maps/`](empathy-maps) — Empathy maps built from the personas above.
- [`prototypes/`](prototypes) — UI prototypes and flows (links to Figma, exported screens, or code prototypes).
- [`design-system/`](design-system) — Tessera's design system: tokens, components, patterns.
- [`brand/`](brand) — Brand identity assets, including [`brand/logos/`](brand/logos).
- [`company-profile/`](company-profile) — Fictional company profile for Tessera (mission, market, product suite, org chart, etc.).
- [`research/`](research) — Supporting research artifacts (interview notes, competitive analysis, etc.) that inform the above.
