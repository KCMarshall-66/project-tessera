Supporting research (interview notes, competitive analysis, etc.) that informs personas and empathy maps.

**Caveats worth knowing before quoting it:** the Gartner Magic Quadrant is paywalled, so only four Leaders were confirmed (via vendor announcements) and others may exist; G2 and Gartner Peer Insights block direct access, so review counts come from secondary sources (mostly a competitor's comparison article) and are tagged accordingly. Figures change quarterly and should be re-verified against the listed sources.
