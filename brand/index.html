Brand

The logo files and product marks behind Tessera. The mark is a cube of tiles with nodes on its vertices: a tessera is a mosaic tile, and the nodes are the vendors connected to it.

Logo and marks

Each asset is shown on a light and a dark surface, because the vector files were authored for the dark navigation rail. Check contrast before using one on white.

Primary logo and dark lockup

Left: the full-color raster logo (tessera-color-logo.png), the source the palette was sampled from. Right: the mark, wordmark and tagline stacked as they appear on the dark nav and the index page. Tagline: Intelligent Third-party Governance.

Mark

The cube alone, full detail. Vector, exported from the Figma nav component (node 7:370). Use above about 90px.

Condensed mark

A re-drawn variant with fewer facets, for small icon-only uses such as the collapsed nav rail and the browser tab icon. Use below about 90px.

Wordmark

"TESSERA" in the brand gray. It is light, so it reads on dark and is too faint on white for anything but decoration.

Tagline

"Intelligent Third-party Governance." First surfaced in the nav component in Figma (node 7:314).

Balanced cube

The full mark with all six node connections spaced evenly. Used as the hub in the Data Mapping flow diagram, where a symmetric layout reads better at small size.

balanced-cube.svg

AI sparkle

The mark for anything Tessera AI does. It opens the chat on every dashboard card, and marks the chat itself. One size, 16px, in the accent blue (#689AE2) so it works on white and on dark.

ai-sparkle.svg

Where each one is used

  • Mark, wordmark, tagline: the sidebar lockup (the mark and condensed mark animate as the nav collapses), and the index page header.
  • Condensed mark: the collapsed nav rail, and the tab icon on the design system and brand pages.
  • Balanced cube: the hub of the Data Mapping diagram and the Signal Flow on Monitoring & Alerts.
  • AI sparkle: the button in the corner of each dashboard card, and the avatar for Tessera AI in the chat.
